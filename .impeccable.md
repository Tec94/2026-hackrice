## Design Context

The owner requested a full replacement using `design_system.pdf` and
`Chartroom Screens.html`. The prior polish-only direction is superseded.

Chartroom is a historical SOL/USDT study room. The HTML's bench workspace,
feedback column, reveal, record, and mobile sheets define the current screens.
The PDF defines lit surfaces, recessed fields/coach, gold actions, copper replay
and refusal cues, violet voice, and restrained state-driven motion.

Use `docs/design-system.md` and `/design-system` as the implementation reference.
The owner chose reversible archive and the existing exact-unchanged Flat rule.
Do not treat sample prices, 80/20 proportions, or the mockup's undecided
±0.25% band as backend facts or product limits.

Keep candles, prices, and chart geometry static during transitions. Fact chips
must come from validated server replies and retain their snapshot identity.
