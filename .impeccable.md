## Design Context

This context carries forward the owner's request to preserve the current look
and apply the supplied surface reference. It is not a new brand direction.

### Users

People practicing numerical chart analysis in a public hackathon MVP. They
explore historical replay data, ask a voice coach for calculations, and form
their own conclusions. The interface must not imply a recommended trade.

### Brand personality

Preserve the existing dark, chart-first interface and restrained amber emphasis.
The proposed shorthand is calm, precise, and tactile; that wording is pending
owner feedback. The owner-supplied surface treatment is confirmed: a 0.5px
border, soft inner white shadow, and double drop shadow.

### Aesthetic direction

Keep the current typeface, neutral surfaces, amber brand accent, and violet
voice accent. Add subtle depth to actionable or floating surfaces, not every
content block. Do not change the chart's colors or trading semantics.

### Design principles

Use these principles when polishing the interface:

- Keep chart data stable; animate interface state, not financial meaning.
- Separate related controls with space before adding containers.
- Preserve visible labels, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Use CSS for these effects; do not add an animation runtime.
- Disable scroll-boundary bounce without disabling normal scrolling or zoom.
